Your CIBIL score is a three-digit number between 300 and 900 that summarises how you have handled credit. Most lenders prefer a score of **700 or more** for unsecured loans, and the best interest rates usually go to borrowers above 750.

The good news: your score is not fixed. Here is what actually moves it.

## 1. Pay every EMI and card bill on time

Payment history is the single biggest factor. Even one payment that is 30 days late can pull your score down for months. Set up auto-debit for at least the minimum amount due.

## 2. Keep credit card usage below 30%

If your total card limit is ₹1,00,000, try to keep your outstanding balance under ₹30,000 when the statement is generated. High utilisation signals that you depend on credit.

## 3. Don’t apply to many lenders at once

Every formal loan application can trigger a "hard enquiry". Several enquiries in a short period suggest credit hunger. Compare lenders first — that is exactly what MYST Finance is for — and then apply to the one or two that fit best.

## 4. Check your credit report for errors

You are entitled to a free credit report every year from each credit bureau. Look for loans you never took, closed accounts still shown as active, or wrong late-payment entries, and raise a dispute with the bureau.

## 5. Keep old accounts open

The age of your credit history matters. Closing your oldest credit card can shorten your history and reduce your total available limit.

## 6. Maintain a healthy credit mix

A mix of secured credit (like a gold or vehicle loan) and unsecured credit (cards, personal loans), handled well, looks better than only one type.

## 7. Be patient

Positive changes usually show up within 30–45 days as lenders report to the bureaus, but rebuilding after a default takes longer. Consistency wins.
