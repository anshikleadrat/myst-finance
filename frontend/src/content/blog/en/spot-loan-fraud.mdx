Digital lending has made credit faster than ever — and fraudsters have followed. These are the warning signs we see most often.

## 1. "Guaranteed approval" for an upfront fee

No regulated lender guarantees a loan before assessing you. If someone asks for a "processing", "insurance" or "file charge" to release a pre-approved loan, stop. Genuine lenders deduct processing fees from the disbursed amount and show them in the Key Fact Statement.

## 2. The lender isn’t registered

Every lender must be a bank or an RBI-registered NBFC, or partner with one. Ask for the NBFC’s name and check it on the RBI website before you share documents.

## 3. Requests for your OTP, PIN or remote access

Nobody needs your bank OTP, UPI PIN or a screen-sharing app to give you a loan. Sharing them can empty your account.

## 4. Apps that want your contacts and photos

Predatory loan apps request access to contacts and galleries, then use them to harass borrowers. A legitimate lender’s app asks only for what KYC requires.

## 5. Pressure to decide immediately

"Offer valid for 10 minutes" is a pressure tactic. Real offers give you time to read the terms, and RBI rules give you a cooling-off period to exit a digital loan.

## 6. No written terms

You should always receive a Key Fact Statement showing the annual percentage rate (APR), all fees and the repayment schedule **before** you sign.

## If something feels wrong

Stop all payments, keep screenshots, report it to your bank, file a complaint at the national cybercrime portal ([cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in), helpline 1930), and alert RBI through [sachet.rbi.org.in](https://sachet.rbi.org.in).
