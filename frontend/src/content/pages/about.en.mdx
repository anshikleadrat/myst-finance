MYST Finance was built for a simple problem: finding the right lender in India means visiting dozens of websites, re-entering the same details and trying to compare rates that are presented differently everywhere.

## What we do

We maintain a curated directory of NBFCs and fintech lenders across personal, business, gold and education loans. You tell us about yourself and the loan you need **once**, and we show you which lenders fit your amount and purpose — with direct links to apply on each lender’s official website.

## What we don’t do

- We are **not a lender**, bank, NBFC or loan agent.
- We do **not** decide loan approvals, interest rates or fees — the lender does.
- We will **never** ask for your bank login, card PIN, UPI PIN or the OTPs your bank sends you.
- We do **not** sell your personal data.

## How we make money

We charge a one-time fee of ₹199 for lifetime access to the full directory with direct links and matching. We do not charge lenders to be listed, so the order of the list is based on fit, not payment.

## Our commitment to accuracy

Lender terms change often. Every lender page shows when its details were last verified, and figures are marked as indicative until checked against the lender’s official Key Fact Statement. If you spot something out of date, please write to [support@mystfinance.in](mailto:support@mystfinance.in).
