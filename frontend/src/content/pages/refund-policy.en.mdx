The ₹199 fee gives instant, lifetime access to our lender directory. Because the digital service is delivered immediately, the fee is generally **non-refundable** once access has been granted.

## When we will refund you

We will refund the full amount if:

- you were **charged more than once** for the same account;
- your payment succeeded but **access was not unlocked** and we cannot fix it within 2 working days;
- the payment was made **without your authorisation** (please also contact your bank).

## Failed payments

If a payment fails but money was debited from your account, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within 5–7 working days. If it is not, contact us with your order ID.

## How to request a refund

Email [support@mystfinance.in](mailto:support@mystfinance.in) (or use the Support page in your dashboard) with your registered email address and order ID. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

## What this fee is not

The fee is not a loan processing fee, is not paid to any lender, and does not guarantee approval of any loan. Lenders’ own fees are charged separately by them and are covered by their refund terms.
