> This policy is a starting template aligned with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before launch.

This Privacy Policy explains how MYST Finance ("we", "us") handles your personal data when you use our website and services.

## Data we collect

| Data | Why we collect it |
| --- | --- |
| Email address | To create your account and send login codes |
| Name, date of birth, city | To match you with suitable lenders |
| PAN | To match lender eligibility criteria; stored encrypted |
| Employment type, employer, income, experience | To match you with suitable lenders |
| Loan amount, purpose, tenure | To match you with suitable lenders |
| Payment records (order ID, amount, method, status) | To provide access, issue invoices and handle refunds |
| Technical data (IP address, browser) | Security, fraud prevention and rate limiting |

We do **not** collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs, bank passwords or the OTPs your bank sends you. Payments are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy.

## How we use your data

- To provide the Service, including showing which lenders fit your application.
- To send login codes, receipts and replies to support requests.
- To keep the Service secure and prevent misuse.
- To comply with legal obligations such as tax record-keeping.

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do not share your application with any lender. When you click through to a lender, you apply on the lender’s website and share data with them directly.

## Consent and your rights

By submitting your application you consent to this processing. You can withdraw consent, ask for a copy of your data, request correction, or ask us to delete your account by emailing [grievance@mystfinance.in](mailto:grievance@mystfinance.in). We may keep payment records for as long as tax law requires.

## Security

Your PAN is encrypted at rest. All traffic uses HTTPS. Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies. Access to production data is restricted to authorised staff.

## Retention

We keep account and application data while your account is active and for up to 3 years after your last activity, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Payment and invoice records are kept for 8 years as required by Indian tax law.

## Grievance Officer

If you have a complaint about how we handle your data, contact our Grievance Officer at [grievance@mystfinance.in](mailto:grievance@mystfinance.in). We will acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

## Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
